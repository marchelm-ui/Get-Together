Get-Together Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Get-Together helps groups of up to 20 people find days and times they are all free.

What we collect

  • A nickname you choose, and the work schedule and free hours you enter. These are shown to the other people on calendars you join — that is the purpose of the app.
  • An anonymous account ID created automatically on your device so your schedule follows you between calendars. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or password.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's advertising policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Where data is stored

Calendar data is stored with Supabase (hosted in the United States) and protected by per-user access rules: only people on the same calendar can see each other's availability.

Deleting your data

Leaving a calendar removes you from it. To delete everything associated with your account, email the address below from within the app's support link and we will remove it within 30 days.

Children

Get-Together is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
